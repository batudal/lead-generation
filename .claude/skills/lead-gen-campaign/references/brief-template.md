# Campaign brief template

Copy into `runs/<run>/brief.md` and fill every field before stage 2. A vague brief produces a vague campaign.

```markdown
# Campaign brief — <slug>

## Thesis / ICP
- Who: <industry, company stage/size, geography>
- Why now: <the trigger/signal that makes them a buyer right now>
- Persona(s): <titles + seniority to target>
- Exclusions: <segments/companies to avoid>

## Offer
- What we're selling: <one line>
- Core value prop: <the outcome the persona cares about>
- Proof: <case study / metric / logo to reference>

## Targets
- Target lead count: <N>
- High-value subset (Sixtyfour deep research): <which accounts/criteria>

## Sequence angle
- Email opener angle: <the hook>
- LinkedIn angle: <connection note angle>
- Channels: [ ] Email (Instantly)  [ ] LinkedIn (HeyReach)
- CTA: <what we want them to do>

## Exa query seeds
- Primary semantic query: "<...>"
- Find-similar seed URLs: <known-good accounts>

## Clay
- Table: <which prebuilt table>
- Enrichment Function: <name>
- Qualification Function: <name + pass criteria>
```
